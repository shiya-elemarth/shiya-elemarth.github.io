Hello World from a fig wasp

2026-11-19

In evolutionary biology, a tiny fig wasp relies on wind currents to carry it across the vast ocean – a distance that would otherwise be impossible to cross.

In the age of AI, a non-technical person can use AI tools to turn ideas into working products – a task that would otherwise be impossible with no engineering team.

Yet being carried by epoch-making wind currents can leave you feeling overwhelmed and lost. Lately, I keep asking myself: when anyone can build an app, what is worth building? And where does my human value fit into it?

So I started this space as an honest place to share my struggles and the process of trying to figure it out. The answer, my friend, is blowin’ in the wind ;)
